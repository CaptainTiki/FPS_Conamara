# Project Conamara — premise

Working title: **Project Conamara**. Boomer shooter. Godot 4.7, TrenchBroom levels, 2D sprite
enemies in a 3D world. Retro textures, modern lighting.

## Where

**Conamara Station, Europa.** Built into the ice near Conamara Chaos, on the hemisphere that
always faces Jupiter. Jupiter fills a third of the sky and never moves. The surface is white-blue
ice cut by rust-brown fracture lines, black sky, no vegetation anywhere on the moon. Gravity is
0.13 g outside the station.

Comms to Earth go through a relay on the far hemisphere. When the relay goes down there is no way
to call home.

## What the project is

Publicly, a research station. Actually, an excavation. Years ago the ice survey found something
kilometres down, and the station has been quietly building *downwards* toward it ever since. The
oldest, most finished modules are at the top (Admin, the Dome, Habitation); the newest and roughest
are at the bottom, where the Tunnel runs out of lining and becomes bare ice and rock with work
lights strung along it. The thing at the bottom is a portal. Nobody has been through it. Nobody
has ever been through it.

## Who you are

The new guard. You arrive on rotation, get a badge, a locker, a tour, and a pistol you are not
expected to use. You arrive on the day they finally reach the thing and switch it on.

## What goes wrong

It opens. Something comes through — a lot of something — and everything electrical on the station
dies with it. You fight *upstream*, restoring power module by module, toward the source. By the
time you reach the portal room the survivors have worked it out: the control systems are slag, and
the only way to close it is from the other side. Nobody says what that means for the guard with
the gun.

You go through.

## Tone

Straight boomer shooter: a series of rooms and corridors, walk through and blast things. Leans
campy over creepy ("should have been creepy but kind of funny"). The station's PA runs on backup
power through the whole thing, welcoming new staff and announcing cafeteria specials to a dead
station. Comedy stays in the environment, not in dialogue, until the game is built.

## Names the crew use

- **Conamara** / **the Station** — the whole base.
- **the Tunnel** — the descent from the finished modules to the portal room. Where they stopped building and kept digging.
- **the Portal Room** — the chamber at the bottom. Also "the Dig" on older signage.
- **through** — the other side. It doesn't get a name; nobody has one for it.
- **the Neighbours** — what the crew call what came through. The PA asked everyone to be considerate.

## Enemies (current)

- **Skitter** — small, fast, melee, in numbers.
- **Drifter** — floating, ranged, aggressive. One per big room.
More come after the full playthrough exists (see levels.md). A level that begs for a boss gets one.
